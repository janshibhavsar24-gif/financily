1️⃣ BUSINESS QUALITY
Q1: What does the company actually do?

Where to find:

U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission EDGAR
https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/

Search company → Open latest 10-K

Look at:

Item 1 → Business

This is the authoritative description.

Q2: How does the company make money?

Where:

10-K → Item 7 → “Management Discussion & Analysis” (MD&A)

Revenue breakdown section

Segment reporting

Also:

10-K → Note 2: Revenue recognition

Q3: What % revenue comes from each segment?

Where:

10-K → “Segment Information”

Financial statement footnotes

Earnings presentation (Investor Relations website)
